## Why not RIGOR MORTIS

High Rive-native technical ceiling, but too much deadline risk from anatomical illustration, rig complexity, IK/constraint behavior, and GPU dependency.

## Why not generic Shadow Puppet

The paper theatre is retained, but the differentiator is specifically temporal disobedience. The project should not rely on the generic title or generic shadow-horror prior art.
