## Central synthesis and choice

**Preferred second build candidate, CONDITIONAL ONLY:** **THE NINTH FOLD** (formerly LA COCOTTE; working name, naming collision still pending).

Positioning: a handmade schoolchild's fortune teller that, upon user-selected colour and number, exposes a forbidden ninth flap that contains impossible space: miniature nested fortune teller stamped with the current user's actual chosen mark/fortune. In the climax the tiny drawn inhabitant opens its own paper toy and the *outer* paper world responds. The **source of horror is spatial recursion and self-referential causality**, not puppet/shadow revolt. No literal OS cursor capture, fake web/browser layer manipulation, or claiming nonexistent real 3D.

**Minimal actor-actions should be 3, not an 8-choice tutorial:**
1. Choose colour and number; the origami opens with real player-linked motion and clearly legible handmade art.
2. An impossible `9` appears where ordinary number sequence ends; choose to open despite caution; inner toy bears *the exact earlier selected mark*.
3. Interact with miniature folded paper; the outer artboard physically changes/folds as a consequence, then permits replay; choices may alter fortunes/inner motif, not just autoplay speed.

Never lose the surprise in a shader technical boast. Full-depth requirement: real consequential choices, multiple valid variations, clear refusal/negative path and recoverable reset, user comprehension and cold link, tactile feel, polished Human Touch evidence. A 15-sec flip animation without user-caused state change is **not** an acceptable final product.

**Backup:** GPT's LAST IMPRESSION **letterpress**, not Perplexity/Gemini darkroom, if primary folding/spatial inversion doesn't meet kill gate. Press->printed world becomes peelable poster; must prove user input affects layers and world reveal, not just scripted stills. Its new interactions and tooling require independent eligibility/novelty checks.

**FACE VALUE retained as alternative only if a demonstrable freehand-shape-to-expressive-rig web slice unexpectedly passes and is materially differentiated from Gourdgeous**; not an unconditional launch.

**THE INDEX OF YOUR VISIT**: high conceptual interest but interaction heavy on reading; the promise of mindreading must be grounded in real observed pointer events, cannot claim precise statistics not captured, no covert tracking of personal information, and must not become another possessed UI. HOLD research only.
